Safety Tips

Book and transact with confidence

Your Safety Comes First

Follow these tips for a smooth and secure service every time.

Quick Safety Checklist

  • ✓ Confirm the provider's verified badge
  • ✓ Agree on price before work begins
  • ✓ Keep all messages inside REPARO
  • ✓ Never share OTPs or passwords
  • ✓ Pay only through the app or after service

Essential Tips

#01

Verify Before You Book

Always look for the green verified badge. It means the provider has a valid business permit and a government-issued ID on file.

#02

Agree on the Price Upfront

Confirm the labor fee and any material costs in the chat before the service starts so there are no surprises when it is done.

#03

Keep Chats Inside the App

Use REPARO chat and calls instead of moving to personal numbers. Your conversation is protected and can support you if a problem comes up.

#04

Check Reviews and Ratings

Read what other customers said about the provider. Consistent high ratings and detailed reviews are the best sign of quality work.

#05

Arrange a Safe Meeting

For home visits, make sure a family member is present. For shop repairs, drop off and pick up during daylight hours in a busy area.

#06

Trust Your Instincts

If a deal feels too good or something seems off, it is okay to walk away. Cancel the booking and report the provider to our team.
