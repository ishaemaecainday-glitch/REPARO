FAQ

Answers to your common questions

BOOKINGS

How do I book a service provider?

Open the Search tab, pick the repair category you need, and choose a provider near you. Tap the booking button, select your preferred date and time, then confirm. You will receive a notification once the provider accepts your request.

BOOKINGS

Can I reschedule or cancel a booking?

Yes. Go to your Booking tab, open the booking you want to change, and tap Reschedule or Cancel. We recommend doing this at least 2 hours before the scheduled service to avoid any cancellation fee.

BOOKINGS

How do I know the provider is on the way?

Once the provider starts traveling, you will get a 'Provider On The Way' notification with their estimated arrival time. You can also chat with them directly from the Messages tab.

PAYMENTS

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept Cash, GCash, Maya, and major Credit or Debit cards. You can choose your preferred payment method during checkout.

PAYMENTS

Do I pay before or after the service?

Payment is placed on hold when you book and is only released to the provider after you confirm that the service has been completed to your satisfaction.

PAYMENTS

How are refunds handled?

If a booking is cancelled by the provider or the service was not delivered, your payment is refunded to your original payment method within 3 to 5 business days. You can track the status in the Booking details screen.

ACCOUNT

How do I update my profile information?

Go to Account Settings, select Edit Profile, update your contact or personal information, and tap Save Changes.

ACCOUNT

How do I change my password?

Navigate to Settings, select Security, and tap Change Password. Enter your current password followed by your new password.

SAFETY

Are the providers verified?

Every provider on REPARO submits a valid business permit and a government-issued ID. Verified providers show a green check badge on their profile so you can book with confidence.
